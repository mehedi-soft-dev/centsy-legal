Privacy Policy

Applies to the Centsy Android app (package com.centsy.app). Last updated: 9 November 2026.

In short: Centsy works entirely on your device. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.

1. What data the app stores

Centsy stores the information you enter yourself — accounts, transactions, categories, loans, notes and app settings — in a private database inside the app's storage on your device. This data never leaves the device unless you explicitly export it.

2. Data we collect

None. Centsy does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. There is no account system, no usage analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising or tracking SDK.

3. Permissions

  • Biometric / fingerprint — used only by your device to unlock the app. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint; verification is handled by Android.
  • Files — used only when you choose to export a report, create a backup or pick a backup folder. The app writes only to the location you select.

4. Backups and exports

Backups are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a password you choose before they are written. They are saved to the folder or location you pick (for example local storage, an SD card or a folder synced by another app). We have no access to those files or to your password.

5. Data sharing

We do not share data with third parties. If you share an exported file or report yourself (for example by email or messaging), that is entirely under your control.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleted transactions are moved to an in-app Trash that you can empty, and uninstalling the app removes all app data. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Security

The app is protected by a PIN (stored as a salted, iterated hash) and optional fingerprint unlock, and its screens are excluded from screenshots and the recent-apps preview. Backups are encrypted before they are saved.

8. Children's privacy

Centsy is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a new “last updated” date.
